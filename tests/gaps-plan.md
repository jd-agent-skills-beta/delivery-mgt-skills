# Supplier Payments Plan

_Calder Print Supplies (fictional organisation; all names in this plan are invented)_

**Plan start date:** 1 November 2026
**Owner:** Finance and Procurement leadership team
**Kind:** delivered

## Result

Our small suppliers are paid within 30 days of sending us a valid invoice.

**By:** spring 2027

## Done when

Over three consecutive calendar months:

1. At least 95% of valid invoices from small suppliers are paid within 30 calendar days of the invoice receipt date.

**Definitions**

- **Small supplier:** a supplier we paid less than £100,000 in total in the 12 months before the measurement period, per the accounts payable ledger.
- **Valid invoice:** an invoice that matches a purchase order and a goods-received note in Paylane, our accounts payable system.
- **Invoice receipt date:** the date Paylane records the invoice as received.
- **Paid:** the payment date recorded in Paylane.
- **Data source:** the Paylane "Days to pay" report, filtered to small suppliers and valid invoices.

**Baseline (August to October 2026):** 58% of valid invoices from small suppliers paid within 30 days.

## Purpose

Small suppliers depend on predictable cash flow. Paying them on time is part of being a good customer, and they are the suppliers least able to absorb late payment.
