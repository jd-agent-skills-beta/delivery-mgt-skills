# Repairs Turnaround Plan

_Fenwick Lettings (fictional organisation; all names in this plan are invented)_

**Plan start date:** 1 October 2026
**Owner:** Priya Shah, Head of Property Services
**Kind:** delivered

## Result

Tenants in the homes we manage get urgent repairs fixed within 24 hours and routine repairs fixed within 10 working days, without the repairs cost per property going up.

**By:** 31 March 2027

## Done when

All measured over 1 January to 31 March 2027:

1. At least 95% of urgent repairs are complete within 24 hours of being logged.
2. At least 90% of routine repairs are complete within 10 working days of being logged.
3. Repairs cost per managed property is no higher than £182, the figure for 1 January to 31 March 2026.

**Definitions**

- **Urgent repair:** a repair logged in one of these RepairDesk categories: no heating, no hot water, water leak, unsafe electrics, door or window that can't be locked. Every other repair is **routine**.
- **Logged:** the "created" timestamp on the job in RepairDesk, our repairs system.
- **Complete:** the job is marked complete in RepairDesk and is not reopened within 14 days.
- **Working day:** Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England.
- **Repairs cost per managed property:** total repair invoices paid in the period, from the finance ledger, divided by the number of properties under management on the last day of the period.
- **Data source:** the RepairDesk "Job turnaround" report and the finance ledger, both run by the Finance Analyst and checked by the owner.

**Baseline (1 July to 30 September 2026):** 61% of urgent repairs complete within 24 hours; 48% of routine repairs within 10 working days; repairs cost £179 per managed property.

## Purpose

Slow repairs are our tenants' biggest complaint: 42% of all complaints in the 12 months to September 2026. An urgent repair left overnight can mean a family without heating or water. Our management fee to landlords is fixed, so getting faster must not cost more per property.

## Activities

| Activity | Live by | Key results |
|---|---|---|
| Triage at logging: tenants choose a category on the repairs form, and the call handler confirms urgent jobs by phone | 15 October 2026 | KR1, KR2, KR4 |
| Weekly turnaround and cost dashboard, reviewed by the owner every Monday | 19 October 2026 | KR1, KR2, KR3, KR4 |
| Fixed-price framework with three contractors for routine jobs | 31 October 2026 | KR2, KR3, KR4 |
| Out-of-hours rota: two contractors on call every evening and weekend for urgent jobs | 1 November 2026 | KR1, KR4 |

Triage goes live first because the rota and the framework both depend on jobs being categorised correctly.

## Constraints

- Use RepairDesk; no new repairs system.
- No additional staff headcount.

## Evidence so far

- A 3-month pilot of an on-call contractor (July to September 2026, 40 properties) completed 88% of urgent repairs within 24 hours.
- Two of the three framework contractors have confirmed in writing that they can take our forecast routine volume at the quoted prices.
- Framework prices average 6% below what we paid per routine job from July to September 2026.
