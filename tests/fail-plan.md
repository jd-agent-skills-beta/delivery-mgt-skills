# Contact Centre Improvement Plan

_Brookmere Energy (fictional organisation; all names in this plan are invented)_

**Owner:** Tom Okafor, Director of Customer Operations

## Result

Customers who contact us get their query resolved faster and with less effort.

**By:** 30 June 2027

## Done when

Customers are noticeably happier with our service, response times are significantly improved, and the contact centre is seen as best in class.

## Purpose

Customer satisfaction has been falling, and we want to be the energy supplier people recommend.

## Workstreams

| Workstream | Dates |
|---|---|
| Launch a chatbot on the website and app | November 2026 to January 2027 |
| Retrain all advisers on the new knowledge base | January to March 2027 |
| Introduce a callback option when queues are long | February 2027 |
| Refresh the contact centre dashboard | March to April 2027 |

## Constraints

- Deliver within the existing contact centre budget.
- Keep current opening hours.
